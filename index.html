01 · Profile

ML systems that are deterministic, well typed, and reliable.

I am a Machine Learning Research Engineer with 7+ years across robotics, computer vision, and generative AI, currently building agentic medical-workflow automation at Waive Medical: document understanding and named-entity recognition for clinical pipelines.

My background begins in competitive robotics: rescue simulation and humanoid soccer at RoboCup, where I first learned to build systems that must perform outside the lab. That path led through a B.Sc. thesis on an embedded deep-learning driver-assistance system, an M.Sc. at Simon Fraser University on probabilistic human navigational intent prediction with MARS Lab and ROSIE Lab, and research with Huawei Canada that produced two ICRA papers on human trajectory and pose prediction.

In industry I have shipped 3D human pose estimation at Hinge Health and a multi-agent analytics engine at Blue Boat Data that converts natural-language questions into guaranteed-correct SQL. Across all of it, my focus is the same: designing ML systems trusted with real-world decisions.
