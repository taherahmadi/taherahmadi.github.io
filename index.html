01 · About

From robotics research
to production AI systems.

My background begins in competitive robotics: rescue simulation and humanoid soccer at RoboCup, where I first learned to build systems that must perform outside the lab. That path led through a B.Sc. thesis on an embedded deep-learning driver-assistance system, an M.Sc. at Simon Fraser University on probabilistic human navigational intent prediction with MARS Lab and ROSIE Lab, and research with Huawei Canada that produced two ICRA papers on human trajectory and pose prediction.

Since then I've shipped ML in production: 3D human pose estimation at Hinge Health, a multi-agent analytics engine at Blue Boat Data that converts natural-language questions into guaranteed-correct SQL, and now agentic medical-workflow automation at Waive Medical: document understanding and NER for clinical pipelines.

Across all of it, my focus is the same: designing ML systems that are deterministic, well-typed, and reliable enough to be trusted with real-world decisions.

Agentic Systems

Multi-agent workflows with typed intermediate representations that eliminate nondeterminism in analytics and clinical automation.

LLM Post-training

Transformers, fine-tuning, and inference infrastructure (vLLM, Hugging Face) for large-scale production ML.

Human Motion & Pose

Trajectory prediction, intent inference with IRL, and real-time 3D pose estimation from monocular RGB.

ML Infrastructure

Scalable pipelines and microservices on AWS/Azure, Docker & K8s, built to survive enterprise traffic.
