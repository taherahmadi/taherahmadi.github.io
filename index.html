Taher Ahmadi

Machine Learning Research Engineer · Vancouver, BC, Canada

I build ML systems that are deterministic, well typed, and reliable: 7+ years across robotics, computer vision, and generative AI. Currently an AI Engineer at Waive Medical, building agentic medical-workflow automation, document understanding, and named-entity recognition for clinical pipelines.

I hold an M.Sc. in Computing Science from Simon Fraser University, where I worked on probabilistic human navigational intent prediction with MARS Lab and ROSIE Lab. Research with Huawei Canada led to two ICRA papers on human trajectory and pose prediction, with 84 citations on Google Scholar. Before that: competitive robotics at RoboCup, including a 2nd place worldwide in the Rescue Simulation league.

A selection of my open-source research and engineering work is on the projects page.
