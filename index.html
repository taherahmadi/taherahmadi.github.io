01 · About

Robots taught me ML.
Now I teach LLMs to behave.

I started in competitive robotics — rescue simulation and humanoid soccer at RoboCup — which is a great way to learn that models fail in the real world long before they fail in a paper. That path led through a B.Sc. thesis on an embedded deep-learning driver-assistance system, an M.Sc. at Simon Fraser University on probabilistic human navigational intent prediction with MARS Lab and ROSIE Lab, and research with Huawei Canada that produced two ICRA papers on human trajectory and pose prediction.

Since then I've shipped ML in production: 3D human pose estimation at Hinge Health, a multi-agent analytics engine at Blue Boat Data that converts natural-language questions into guaranteed-correct SQL, and now agentic medical-workflow automation at Waive Medical — document understanding and NER for clinical pipelines.

The common thread: taking models that are impressive in a demo and making them deterministic, typed, and reliable enough to be trusted with real decisions.

03 · Publications

Peer-reviewed work

Top robotics venues, on how machines understand where humans are going.

ICRA2023

3D Human Pose Prediction With Non-Autoregressive Transformers

M. Mahdavian, P. Nikdel, M. TaherAhmadi, M. Chen

A non-autoregressive transformer that predicts full-body 3D pose and global trajectory jointly — decoding the future in parallel instead of step-by-step, for faster and more accurate long-horizon prediction.

Optimal Control Inspired Probabilistic Model-Based Human Navigational Intent Inference

M. TaherAhmadi*, P. Agand*, M. Chen, A. Lim

Infers where a person intends to go by modeling humans as noisily-optimal planners — combining inverse reinforcement learning with optimal control for probabilistic intent prediction.
